Our Story

A grandmother's kitchen, twice over.

Chef Anaya Moretti grew up between two grandmothers — one in Modena, the other in Jaipur — and an unspoken rivalry over whose pantry held the better story. Saffron & Thyme is her answer: not fusion as collision, but as conversation.

Every plate begins with the same question. What would Nonna say? And then — what would Naani add? The result is a menu that doesn't choose. It simply, finally, agrees.

— Anaya Moretti Executive Chef & Owner
